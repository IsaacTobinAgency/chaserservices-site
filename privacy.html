Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Chaser collects, why, and what your rights are. Chaser is run by a sole trader based in Ireland (1 Horgans Quay, Co. Cork). You can contact us about anything in this policy at hello@chaserservices.ie.

Two kinds of people we hold data about

Our customers are the businesses that sign up to Chaser. For their data, we decide how it is used, so we are the “controller”.

Our customers’ customers are the people who call or message a business that uses Chaser. We handle their data only to reply on that business’s behalf and on its instructions, so for this data we are a “processor” and the business is the controller.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, business name, email address and phone number.
  • Business setup details: your trade, service area, opening hours and the information you give Chaser to answer customers.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We see your plan and payment status, but never your full card number.
  • Caller and customer details: phone numbers, the messages exchanged, and job details people share (for example, the job, address and how urgent it is).
  • Quote details: when a business asks Chaser to chase a quote, the customer's name, email address, phone number and what the quote was for, plus whether the business won the job.
  • Basic technical data when you visit this website, such as your browser type and IP address, used to keep the site working and secure.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for: replying to missed calls, following up quotes by email in the business's name, and sending you summaries. Legal basis: performing our contract with you.
  • To bill you and manage your subscription. Legal basis: contract, and our legal obligations to keep financial records.
  • To support you, fix problems and improve Chaser. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a reliable service.
  • To send you important account and service emails. We will only send marketing if you agree, and you can opt out at any time.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your customers’ data for our own marketing.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run Chaser. They only process data on our instructions:

  • Stripe, for payments.
  • Twilio Ireland Limited, to receive forwarded phone calls.
  • Kapso, a WhatsApp Business Platform provider, which connects a business's own WhatsApp Business number to Chaser so we can send and receive WhatsApp messages on its behalf. The messages themselves are carried by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited).
  • Anthropic (United States), to help write replies to callers and incoming emails. It does not use this data to train its models.
  • n8n GmbH (Germany), which runs our automations, our sign-up form and our customer list.
  • Zoho Corporation (EU data centre), for our email, including the quote follow-up emails we send on a business's behalf.
  • GitHub, to host this website.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised under EU law, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open and for 12 months after it closes, unless we have to keep it longer by law (for example, financial records). Conversations with callers are deleted automatically 12 months after the last message, and quote records 12 months after the quote was added. Business customers can ask us to delete their customers’ data sooner, at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. Email hello@chaserservices.ie and we will reply within one month. If you are a customer of a business that uses Chaser, you can also contact that business directly.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our fonts are hosted on our own site, so no third party is contacted when you read it. If you pay through Stripe, Stripe sets its own cookies on its payment page, which are needed to process payments securely and prevent fraud.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell customers by email.
