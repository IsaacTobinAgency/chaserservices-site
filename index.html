What happens when you miss a call

Calls you answer work exactly as they do today. Chaser only steps in when you can't pick up.

  1. 0:00

    You miss the call

    You're on a roof, driving or with a customer. The call forwards quietly to your Chaser number.

  2. Seconds later

    They get a WhatsApp

Questions trades ask us

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your own number. Calls you don't answer go to your Chaser number instead of voicemail, so everything else works exactly as it does today.

Whose name shows on the WhatsApp?

Yours. Chaser connects to the WhatsApp Business app you already use, so the caller gets a message from your own number and business name. Every message says it's your automated assistant. You see every chat on your phone and can jump in any time: Chaser steps back as soon as you reply.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

Chaser works with the free WhatsApp Business app on your business mobile. Not using it yet? Switching takes about 5 minutes on the same number, and your chats come with you: here's how. Either way, you hear about every missed call so you can ring back.

Will my customers know it's automated?

Yes. Chaser writes in your business's name and says it's your automated assistant. It never quotes prices or promises times. If a customer asks for a person, you're told straight away.

What about emergencies?

Urgent jobs, like a burst pipe or no heat, come to your phone straight away instead of waiting for the summary. If a caller mentions a gas smell or danger, Chaser gives them the Gas Networks Ireland emergency number and 112/999 at once.

How does call forwarding work?

Your Chaser number is an ordinary Irish landline number (021), so forwarding to it costs the same as calling any Irish landline on your plan. Most Irish mobiles can forward calls you don't answer. It also covers calls when you're busy, out of signal or switched off, and it replaces your voicemail while it's on. We email you the exact code with your Chaser number. You type it in once and press call. To switch it off, dial ##004#. If your network needs a different code, just reply to our email and we'll send it.

What if a caller doesn't use WhatsApp?

Chaser tells you if the WhatsApp couldn't be delivered, so you can give them a ring back yourself. Callers who withhold their number can't be messaged, so ring those back as you normally would.

How does quote chasing work?

You get a private "Chase a quote" link with your Chaser number. Add the customer, the job and when you sent the quote, and Chaser emails them up to three polite follow-ups in your name. Replies go straight to you. Tap "We won it", "They said no" or "Stop chasing" whenever you like. Only add customers who asked you for a quote.

What happens after the free days?

A few days before your trial ends, we email you a link to keep Chaser running. If you don't add a card, Chaser pauses and you're never charged.

Is my customers' data safe?

We only use your customers' details to reply to them and chase quotes on your behalf. We never sell them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. Conversations are deleted automatically after 12 months. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Can I cancel?

Any time, with no fees and no contract. Reply to any email from Chaser, or use the link in your receipt.
