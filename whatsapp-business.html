Chaser works with WhatsApp Business

Chaser replies to your missed callers from your own number, through the free WhatsApp Business app. Here's how to check which WhatsApp you have, and how to switch in about 5 minutes if you need to.

Which WhatsApp do I have?

You're ready

WhatsApp Business

The app icon has a B in the speech bubble, and Settings shows Business tools. Nothing to change.

Switch first

Normal WhatsApp

The icon has a phone handset in the bubble, and there's no Business tools in Settings. Follow the steps below.

Switch to WhatsApp Business (same number, same chats)

  1. Back up your chats. In WhatsApp, go to Settings, then Chats, then Chat backup, and tap Back up. iPhones back up to iCloud, Android phones to your Google account.
  2. Install WhatsApp Business. It's free on the App Store and Google Play, made by WhatsApp.
  3. Use the same number. Open WhatsApp Business and enter your business mobile number. When it offers to restore your chats, say yes.
  4. Add your business name. This is the name your customers see. You can add your hours and area too.
  5. Check your chats are there, then you can delete the old WhatsApp app.

Good to know

  • A number can only be on one WhatsApp app at a time. After switching, use WhatsApp Business for that number.
  • Your customers won't notice any difference, except that they'll see your business name.
  • If you've just switched, use the app for about a week before we connect Chaser. Meta asks for the app to have been in use for a while first.
  • Keep WhatsApp Business on your phone and open it at least every couple of weeks, or the connection pauses. We'll remind you.

Don't use WhatsApp on your work phone? Tell us when you sign up. We have another option, and we'll talk you through it.
